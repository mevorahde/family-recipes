## Ingredients

- 6 to 7 apples, peeled and sliced
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 pie crusts
- 2 tablespoons butter

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Toss the sliced apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice.
3. Roll out the bottom crust and place it in a pie dish.
4. Fill the crust with the apple mixture and dot with butter.
5. Cover with the top crust, seal the edges, and cut a few slits in the top.
6. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until the crust is golden and the filling is bubbling.
7. Cool before serving for the best slices.

## Notes

A little vanilla ice cream makes it feel like Sunday supper at Grandma's house.
